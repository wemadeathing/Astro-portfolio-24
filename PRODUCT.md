# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose
nasifsalaam.com is the portfolio and sales site of Nasif Salaam, an independent product designer and AI builder in Cape Town. Its job is to win freelance projects: websites and brand work for SMBs, AI products and MVPs for founders and teams, overflow capacity for agencies, and AI training for organisations (via ai.nasifsalaam.com). The portfolio is a conversion tool for outreach, not an employment showcase.

## Register
brand

## Users
- **Skimmers**: SMB owners and referrers who arrive from a WhatsApp or email link and want to see "what websites has this guy done" in under a minute. Mostly mobile.
- **Readers**: founders, product leads, and agency principals evaluating depth: how he thinks, how he works, whether he can own a build end to end. Mostly desktop.
- Both audiences reach case studies from cold/warm outreach links, the work grid, or the AI chat.

## Brand & Tone
- Positioning: "Product Designer + AI Builder", one person from brief to working product, speed with craft (App Store in 30 days, brand + site in 3 weeks).
- Voice: plain, confident, specific. Proof over adjectives. No hype.
- Voice bans: never the word "shipped"; no em dashes anywhere in copy.
- Visual identity (existing, committed): dark-first theme, near-black background with light text, single primary accent used sparingly, Geist Mono uppercase kickers/labels with wide tracking, hairline borders (border/60-80), softly rounded corners (--radius, 0.75rem; the site moved off square corners in 2026-09), diagonal-stripe motifs (btn-stripe, frame-stripe), scroll-reveal entrances. The site already has a voice; case studies should deepen it, not replace it.

## Strategic Principles
- Every case study must work at two scroll speeds: images + captions alone tell the problem-solution story for skimmers; the narrative column rewards readers.
- Proof beats polish: outcomes, timelines, live links. Every claim concrete and honest (attribution for past-job work).
- Case studies are sales pages: each ends with a path to contact/booking.
- Curation over volume: 9 pieces, each earning its card (real screenshots, live links where they exist, one outcome line).

## Anti-references
- Blog-post-shaped case studies (uniform text river with inline images).
- Generic SaaS portfolio templates: identical card grids, hero-metric blocks, gradient text.
- Dribbble-style eye candy with no substance, or agency sites that hide the work behind mood.
